Why Koh Samui?

Thailand's premier family island — where paradise meets practicality

☀️
Year-Round Sunshine
Warm tropical climate on the Gulf of Thailand. Average 28-32°C all year with a mild rainy season from November to January.
🌎
International Community
15+ nationalities represented. A thriving expat community with families from Russia, Europe, Australia, and Asia.
🏥
Modern Hospitals
Bangkok Hospital Samui and Thai International Hospital provide world-class healthcare with English-speaking doctors.
✈️
Direct Flights
Daily flights to Bangkok (1 hour), plus direct connections to Singapore, Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, and Chiang Mai.
💰
Lower Cost of Living
30-40% more affordable than Phuket and Bangkok. Quality family life without the big-city price tag.
👪
Safe & Family-Friendly
One of Thailand's safest islands. Low crime rate, clean beaches, and a relaxed pace of life ideal for raising children.

Visa Options for Thailand

Multiple pathways to long-term residence on Koh Samui

🎓
Education Visa (ED)
Available for children enrolled in a Thai school. Parents can obtain a guardian visa. Renewable annually — the most popular option for families.
⭐
Thailand Elite Visa
Premium residence program: 5, 10, or 20-year options. No work permit needed. VIP airport service, fast-track immigration.
💼
Business / Work Visa
For entrepreneurs and employees of Thai companies. Requires a work permit and BOI or company registration.
🌴
Retirement Visa (50+)
For those aged 50 and over. Proof of 800,000 THB in a Thai bank or 65,000 THB/month income. Renewable annually.
