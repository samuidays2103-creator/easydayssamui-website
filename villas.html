Buying Property on Koh Samui as a Foreigner

Everything you need to know about legal ownership in Thailand

📜
Leasehold vs Freehold
Leasehold (30 years, renewable) is the only fully legal way for foreigners to own property in Thailand. Our villas come with a registered lease agreement at the Land Department.
🏠
Villa in Your Name
The villa is registered in the foreign buyer's name with a construction permit and lease agreement officially recorded with the Land Department.
💪
No Nominee Schemes
We use only transparent, law-compliant ownership structures. No Thai nominees, no shell companies — full legal protection for your investment.
🏞
Koh Samui vs Phuket
Comparable villa quality at 40-50% lower prices than Phuket. Calmer lifestyle, less tourist congestion, and strong rental demand year-round.
📈
Property Value Growth
Leasehold villas on Koh Samui appreciate 25-30% upon completion, with annual growth of approximately 7%. Strong demand for ready-to-move-in properties.
